# Fine, I'll Follow: Prologue

I've made a mistake. I wish I could say I was sad about the death of the dragons or the Elves, but for all their superiority and wisdom, you'd think they'd have improved the essential skill of diplomacy. Somehow, advanced knowledge of the concept of "debt" defeated their depth of knowledge of the ways of magic. Understanding the metaphysical trumped their pursuit of the physical and so an undergraduate physics student embarassed them all. Funny. They could call lightning down from the heavens, spit fire, summon rocks, ghosts, or demons, but couldn't structure a deal to facilitate tangible growth.
